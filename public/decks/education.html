01 — OVERVIEW

The Complete Picture

A unified AI platform that brings order to educational chaos.

Nestora is an AI-driven household operations platform that organizes school communication, assignments, forms, deadlines, class schedules, and documents for families. It integrates seamlessly with the tools families already use to create a unified command center.

By connecting parent email, student accounts, and learning management systems, Nestora creates a single source of truth for every family's educational journey.
